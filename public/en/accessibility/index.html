Accessibility statement

Our commitment

ExitCube believes in equal, accessible service for everyone and works to make this site accessible in the spirit of Israeli Standard 5568 and WCAG 2.0 level AA.

What we have done

The site adapts to phones and computers; it can be navigated by keyboard; focus is visible; text has high contrast and can be enlarged; images have text alternatives; and animations respect the device's reduce-motion setting.

Known limitations

The logo film is decorative, silent and carries no essential information. Images on the site are concept art. Some parts of the site may not be fully accessible yet, and we keep improving them.

Accessibility of the service

If a child has special needs, tell us in advance and we will adapt the game as far as we can.

Contact about accessibility
